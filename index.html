Top 10 Roller Coasters

10. Hakugei

Hakugei is and RMC coaster that opened in March 28, 2019. It is located in Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Japan. It is a hybrid coaster meaning it is made of wood and steel. It has a height of 180 feet, is 5020 feet long, and hits speeds of up to 66 mph. It features 3 inversions, (a zero G stall and two zero G rolls) a first drop height of approximately 160-170 feet at 80 degrees!

9.Stardust Racers (Green)

Stardust Racers Green is a dueling coaster located in Universal Epic Universe in Orlando, Florida. It is a steel coaster by Mack rides. It first opened to the public on May 22, 2025. The ride stands at 135 feet, reaches speeds of 62 mph, and is 5000 feet long. The ride features one inversion, a first drop of 125 feet at 80-85 degree angle, duel LMS launches, and the "Celestial spin" which is the coasters signature element and inversions where the two tracks cross over each other.

8. Eejanaika

Eejanaika is a S&S sansei steel coaster. It opened on July 19, 2006 at Fuji-Q Highland in Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi, Japan. It has a height of 249 feet, a length of 3783 feet, and reaches 78 mph. It feutures a first drop of 213 feet at 89 degrees, nearly verticle! It is one of only three 4D coasters in the world, one of them being X2 which is currently closed due to pending investigations. A 4D coaster is a coaster were the seats rotate a full 360 degrees. It is able to do this because a inovative 4 rail track. In adition to the 11 seat rotation, the ride also features 3 track rotations, a inside raven turn, an zero G roll, and an outside raven turn.

7. Ride to Happieness

The Ride to Happiness by Tomorrowland is a world-renowned Mack Rides Xtreme Spinning Coaster that opened on July 21, 2021, at Plopsaland De Panne in Belgium. It stands 115 feet tall, reaches a top speed of 56 mph, and stretches nearly 3,000 feet in length. The coaster is highly celebrated for its five inversions—including a slow-rolling JoJo roll out of the station—and two powerful magnetic launches. Because its free-spinning cars rotate independently based on rider weight distribution and have no spin-dampening magnets, the chaotic, high-intensity experience is completely different on every single ride.
